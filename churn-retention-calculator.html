      Where churn usually comes from

      • The first weeks. Customers who never get the first result they came for leave early. Look at churn by joining month, not only in total.
      • Service failures. A missed delivery, a long queue or an unanswered complaint. These show up in support logs before they show up in churn.
      • Value for money. When the price rises or a cheaper option appears. Ask the customers who leave, and check your price increase math first.

      Questions

      How do you calculate churn rate?

      Divide the customers lost during a period by the customers you had at the start of it. Losing 100 of 2,000 customers in a month is a 5% monthly churn rate.

      What is the difference between customer churn and revenue churn?

      Customer churn counts people; revenue churn counts money. Losing a few large accounts can mean low customer churn but high revenue churn, so track both.

      How does churn affect customer lifetime value?

      The average customer lifetime is roughly 1 divided by monthly churn. Halving churn roughly doubles how long a customer stays, and the lifetime value with it.
