      How to run the test so it means something

      • Write the questions your buyers ask, not the ones you hope for: “best clinic for diabetes care in Dhaka”, “which payroll software suits a 50-person firm”. Ten is a start; twenty is better.
      • Use a fresh session for each engine, signed out where you can, so past chats do not tilt the answer.
      • Count a mention only when you are recommended or listed, not when you appear in a warning or a footnote.
      • Repeat monthly with the same list. Answers move; the trend matters more than one reading.

      Two markets, two languages

      In the US, ask in English. In Bangladesh, ask the same questions in Bangla and in English, because buyers search both ways and the engines often answer them differently. A brand that is named in English but missing in Bangla has found its first job. The fixes are the same in both: answer the question plainly on your own site, be named on the sources the engines cite, and keep your facts consistent everywhere they appear.       Questions

      What is share of answer?

      The share of AI answers to your buyers’ questions that name your brand. Ask the same questions in each engine, count the answers that recommend or list you, and divide by the number of questions asked.

      How do you measure brand visibility in ChatGPT and other AI engines?

      Write 10 to 20 questions a real buyer would ask, put each one to ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews in a fresh session, record whether you and your top rival are named, and repeat monthly with the same list.

      How do you increase your share of answer?

      Answer your buyers’ questions plainly on your own site, near the top of the page, get named on the sources the engines cite, and keep your name, services and facts consistent across your site and profiles.
