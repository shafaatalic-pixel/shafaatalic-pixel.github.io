      Why pacing matters

      Underspending leaves reach on the table and can cost you the media rates you negotiated. Overspending burns the budget early and leaves the campaign dark at the end, often just when it matters most. Check pacing at least weekly, daily for short or high-budget flights.

      The calculator assumes an even daily plan. If your plan is front-loaded or weighted to weekends, compare against that plan instead.

      Questions

      What is campaign pacing?

      How actual spend compares with the planned spend for the time that has passed. 100% means exactly on plan.

      What pacing range is acceptable?

      Within about 10% either side of plan is a common working tolerance. Larger gaps need a decision.

      Why might a campaign underspend?

      Narrow audiences, low bids, ads waiting for approval, frequency caps, or budget set at a level the platform cannot deliver.
