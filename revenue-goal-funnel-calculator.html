      How to use the plan

      • Use real rates, not hopes. Take conversion rates from the last 3 to 6 months. If you have none, start conservative and update after the first month.
      • Find the weakest step. Doubling traffic doubles cost. Improving a weak conversion step often costs far less and compounds through the rest of the funnel.
      • Check the budget against value. If the media budget per customer is close to what a customer is worth, the plan does not work at any scale. See the LTV to CAC calculator.

      Questions

      What is a reverse funnel calculation?

      It starts from the revenue you want and works backwards through each conversion rate to the customers, leads and visitors needed to get there.

      What conversion rates should I use?

      Your own recent averages. Industry benchmarks vary too widely by sector, price point and channel to be reliable for planning.

      Does this include repeat purchases?

      Only if you enter a revenue per customer that includes them, for example first-year value instead of a single order.
