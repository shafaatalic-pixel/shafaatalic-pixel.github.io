      How to plan a surge you can see coming

      • Find your date, not just your peak. The number that matters is the day your beds run out. That is the deadline for converting wards, calling in staff and opening outpatient follow-up.
      • Length of stay is a lever. Beds in use are roughly admissions a day times days in bed. Clear protocols for who goes home and when free beds faster than building them.
      • Run the worse case. Halve the doubling time and see whether you still hold. Plans that only work on the hopeful curve fail in August.
      • Late arrival is the killer. For dengue, most deaths follow late presentation. Teaching the warning signs before the peak cuts both deaths and the sickest admissions.

      Dengue shows why the date matters

      Bangladesh recorded 114,251 dengue admissions and 352 deaths in 2026 by 19 October, and at the height of the season one Dhaka hospital had 145 dengue patients for 38 beds. The season peaks on roughly the same calendar every year, which makes it plannable. The same math applies to a flu season in a US hospital, a measles outbreak in a county or a heatwave in a city.       Questions

      How do you calculate hospital beds needed in an outbreak?

      Estimate admissions a day at the peak, from daily cases times the share admitted, then multiply by the average length of stay. For 30 admissions a day and a 4-day stay, you need about 120 beds at once. This planner projects cases forward from the doubling time and adds up the admissions still in bed each day.

      What is a doubling time?

      The number of days it takes for daily cases to double. A doubling time of 7 days means cases grow about 10% a day. You can estimate it from your own counts: if cases went from 20 to 40 a day in 10 days, the doubling time is 10 days.

      How many nurses do I need in a surge?

      Divide the patients in bed at the peak by the patients one nurse can safely care for in a shift, then multiply by the shifts in a day. For 120 patients at 6 per nurse, that is 20 nurses a shift and about 60 across three shifts, before days off.
