      Why paperwork, not eligibility, decides most losses

      • The unwinding showed it. When pandemic Medicaid protections ended, about 25 million people were disenrolled, and about 69% of them for procedural reasons such as an unreturned form, not because they were found ineligible.
      • Automatic renewal is the cheapest outreach. About 61% of people who kept Medicaid were renewed from data the state already held. Every point you add there is a person who never needs a call.
      • Reach is not completion. Count the people who finish the step after you contact them, not the letters you sent.
      • Work the deadline backwards. With a 30-day notice window, the staff you need is set by the days you have, not the year.

      The same problem in Bangladesh and the US

      In the US the paperwork is a Medicaid renewal or a work-hours check. In Bangladesh it is a stipend or allowance re-verification, a mobile-wallet account check or a national ID match. The fix is the same in both: match the data you already have, flag the people who are exempt, send one plain instruction with one deadline, and send a trusted person to the ones who do not respond.       Questions

      Why do people lose Medicaid at renewal?

      Mostly for paperwork. During the Medicaid unwinding, about 25 million people were disenrolled, and about 69% of those were for procedural reasons, such as a form not returned in time, rather than being found ineligible.

      What is ex parte renewal?

      Renewing someone automatically using data the agency already holds, such as wage, tax or SNAP records, so the person does not have to send anything. About 61% of people who kept Medicaid during the unwinding were renewed this way.

      How many outreach workers do I need for a renewal drive?

      Multiply the people who must act by the contact attempts per person, then divide by the contacts one worker can make a day times the working days in the window. For 30,000 people, 3 attempts, 60 contacts a day and 22 working days, that is about 68 workers.
