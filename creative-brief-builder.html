        What makes a brief work

        A brief is a creative tool, not a form. Its job is to make the problem clear and the opportunity exciting, in a page. The proposition is the heart of it: one thought, sharp enough that a creative team can do something surprising with it.

        • Insight, not observation. "Parents are busy" is an observation. The insight is the tension underneath it. Try the insight statement builder.
        • Fewer reasons to believe. Two strong proofs beat six weak ones.

        Questions

        What should a creative brief include?

        The business problem, the audience, an insight, a single-minded proposition, reasons to believe, tone, what you want people to think, feel and do, channels, mandatories, success measures, timing and budget.

        What is a single-minded proposition?

        The one thing you want people to take away, in a short sentence. If it needs an "and", it is probably two propositions.

        How long should a brief be?

        One page. If it does not fit, the thinking usually is not finished.
