      Read a growth rate carefully

      • The base. Growth from a small base looks fast. Doubling 10 customers is easier than adding 10% to 10,000.
      • Price or volume. Revenue can grow because prices rose while customers stayed flat. Check both.
      • Seasons and one-offs. Compare the same season each year, and take out one large deal before calling it a trend.

      Questions

      How do you calculate CAGR?

      Divide revenue now by revenue at the start, raise it to the power of 1 over the number of years, and subtract 1. Growing from 1 million to 2.1 million over two years is about 45% a year.

      How do monthly and yearly growth rates relate?

      They compound. 3% a month is about 43% a year, not 36%, because each month grows on top of the last.

      What is a good growth rate?

      It depends on your stage and market. A useful test is to compare it with how fast your market grows: growing faster means you are taking share.
