What the fifteen questions check

One question for each thing a growth team must have in place, grouped the way the Growth operating system is grouped. Each answer scores 0 to 3; the score is your total out of 45, shown as a percentage.

Plan

  • Market size. Do you know how big your reachable market really is? Market size calculator
  • Target to funnel. Is this year's revenue target broken down into the customers, leads and visitors you need? Revenue goal funnel planner
  • Positioning. Can everyone on the team say in one sentence what you stand for that competitors do not? Brand positioning ladder
  • Customer insight. Is your marketing built on a customer insight you can state, with the tension inside it? Consumer insight statement builder
  • Brand vs activation. Do you decide how much goes to brand building versus activation (short-term sales)? Marketing budget planner

Execute by channel

  • CAC by channel. Do you know what a new customer costs you in each channel, including people and tools? CAC by channel calculator
  • Pacing. Mid-campaign, do you know whether you are over or under spending against plan? Campaign pacing calculator
  • Creative brief. Does every piece of work start with a one-page brief with a single proposition? Creative brief builder
  • Content program. Is your content planned around what customers need at each stage, with owners and a cadence? Content marketing scorecard
  • Idea screening. Before spending on a new idea, do you score it against a fixed set of tests? Marketing idea scorecard

Measure and optimize

  • Measurement plan. Do you agree what success looks like, and how you will measure it, before a campaign starts? Campaign measurement plan builder
  • Marketing ROI. When you report marketing ROI, is it based on incremental profit rather than total sales? Marketing ROI calculator
  • LTV to CAC. Do you know your LTV to CAC ratio and how many months it takes a customer to pay back? LTV to CAC calculator
  • Retention leaks. Do you know where customers drop off, and what one point less churn is worth? Customer journey mapper
  • Testing. When you run a test, do you check whether the result is real before acting on it? A/B test significance calculator

What the bands mean

  • Starting (0 to 35). Growth is happening by effort, not by system. Every win depends on someone pushing. The good news: the first fixes are cheap and quick.
  • Building (36 to 60). The pieces exist but they do not yet connect. Money is being spent before the plan and measured after the fact.
  • Running (61 to 80). A working system with a few gaps, and those gaps are where money leaks. Close the weakest stage and the rest compounds.
  • Compounding (81 to 100). A system that learns from its own numbers. Protect it, and keep pushing the weakest stage so it never becomes the ceiling.

Questions people ask

How long does the Growth Readiness Score take?

About two minutes: fifteen multiple-choice questions, five for each stage of the growth system (plan, execute, measure). You see your score and your first three moves straight away, with no sign-up.

What happens to my answers?

Your answers stay in your browser. When you finish, an anonymous copy of the score and the fifteen answers (no name, email or company) is stored so I can publish averages later. You can skip that by not completing the score.

Is the score a benchmark?

No. It is a self-assessment against a checklist of what a working growth system needs. The averages I publish later will show how other teams answered, but your own weakest stage matters more than the comparison.
