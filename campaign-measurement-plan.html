      A chain, not a dashboard

      Good measurement links four levels: people notice the work, it changes what they think or feel, they do something, and the business benefits. Measuring every level shows where the chain breaks. Strong recall with no change in consideration points to the message; strong consideration with no sales points to price, distribution or the buying experience.

      • Choose a few measures per level, and set targets before launch, not after.
      • Allow for time. Brand effects build over months; activation effects show in days.

      Questions

      What should a campaign measurement plan include?

      An objective, and measures with targets at four levels: communication (seen), attitude (felt), behaviour (did) and business (paid off).

      Why not just measure sales?

      Sales are affected by many things. Measuring the earlier steps shows whether the campaign itself worked and where to fix it.

      How many measures should I pick?

      Two or three per level is usually enough. More measures make reporting heavier without adding insight.
