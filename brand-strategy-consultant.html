Brand is the promise the price and the product keep

Most brand work starts with a logo and ends with a launch. The work that moves a number starts earlier: what the company is for, who it is not for, and what the customer is being asked to believe. Creative is the last ten percent. When the positioning is right, the campaign gets cheaper and the sales conversation gets shorter, because the brand has already done the arguing.

  • Positioning and brand strategy. The category you are in, the claim you can own, and the price that proves it. Create the category, don't compete in one.
  • Brand launches and relaunches. From a hospital repositioned from "COVID testing centre" to "quality healthcare", lifting footfall by roughly 190 percent, to a beauty and wellness launch with a national brand ambassador covered in Prothom Alo.
  • Integrated campaigns. Multi-channel product campaigns where retail, digital, PR and pricing move together, the way the Galaxy Note9 launch did.
  • Thought leadership and executive visibility. Institutional and executive positioning in the media that matters to your buyers, the way ACCA's professional-audience programme reached national press. The personal-brand playbook.
  • Awareness at population scale. When the audience is ten million people, most of them rural and low-literacy, awareness is a distribution and trust problem before it is a creative one.
The best campaign is the one you no longer need, because the positioning did its job. Everything below was built on that principle.

Communications that also change behaviour

The same discipline sits behind a flagship launch and a government stipend rollout: understand what the audience already believes, find the one thing that has to change, and build every channel around it. I carry a Master of Public Health alongside the marketing credentials, which is why the work holds up when the audience is patients, beneficiaries or regulators rather than shoppers. Health communication is the same craft with higher stakes.

Questions people ask before we talk

Do you do creative and design?

I set the positioning, the message architecture and the campaign system, and I work with your agency, designers or in-house team on execution. If you need an agency, I will help you brief and choose one.

Brand or growth first?

Positioning first, always. A growth plan built on unclear positioning spends money arguing with the market. Once the brand is doing the arguing, growth gets cheaper.

Do you work on personal or executive brand?

Yes, as thought leadership: the ideas an executive should be known for, where they should appear, and how the programme compounds. It is the same work I did for ACCA at institutional scale.

Do you work in both the United States and Bangladesh?

Yes. Agency roots and most of the campaign record are from Dhaka; I am based in Michigan now and work with brands on both sides, including brands crossing from one market to the other.

What does the first month look like?

A positioning diagnosis: how customers, competitors and your own team describe you, where those three disagree, and a written positioning and message architecture your next campaign can be briefed from.
