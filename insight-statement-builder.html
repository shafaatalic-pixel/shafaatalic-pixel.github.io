        Observation is not insight

        "Parents are busy" is an observation. An insight explains why people behave the way they do, and it almost always contains a tension: they want one thing, but something stops them. That tension is what a brand can resolve, and what makes an idea feel true when people see it.

        • The test: when you read it to someone in the audience, do they say "that's so true"?

        Questions

        What is a consumer insight?

        A fresh understanding of why people think or behave the way they do, usually built from an observation, an underlying motivation and a tension that gets in the way.

        How do I find insights?

        Watch and listen to people: interviews, reviews, social conversations, sales calls, behaviour data. Look for contradictions between what people say and what they do.

        Why does an insight need a tension?

        Because the tension is the problem the brand can solve. Without it, the statement is just a description.
