      Questions

      How do you calculate the break-even point?

      Divide fixed costs by the contribution per unit, which is price minus variable cost. 20,000 of fixed costs at 20 contribution per unit means 1,000 units.

      What is margin of safety?

      How far sales can fall before you make a loss, as a share of expected sales. A higher margin of safety means a more resilient business.

      Should marketing be a fixed or variable cost?

      Brand and salaried marketing are usually fixed; per-sale costs like affiliate fees or payment charges are variable.
