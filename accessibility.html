Accessibility

Accessibility statement

Last updated 13 October 2026

I want everyone to be able to read and use this site, whatever device, browser or assistive technology they use. The goal is to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA.

What is in place

  • Readable structure. Pages use real headings, lists and landmarks, so screen readers can jump through them.
  • Keyboard friendly. Links, buttons and tools work with a keyboard, with a visible focus outline.
  • Less motion on request. If your device is set to reduce motion, the animated backgrounds and entrance effects switch off.
  • Light and dark themes, with text colours chosen for contrast in both.
  • Text alternatives. Images carry descriptions, and the introduction video has captions and a written transcript.
  • Plain language, and a Bangla version of the core profile.

Known limitations

  • Some case-study infographics are images with text in them. The key facts are also written out in the page, but not every detail.
  • The interactive impact map works best with a mouse or touch. Its content is also available as text for search engines and screen readers.

Tell me if something does not work

If any part of the site is hard to use, or you need content in another format, email hello@shafaatalichoyon.com with the page and what happened. I will reply and fix what I can.
