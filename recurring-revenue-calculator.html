      Four ways to grow recurring revenue

      • New customers. The obvious lever, and usually the most expensive. Check what each one costs with the CAC calculator.
      • Expansion. More users, more services or a higher plan for customers who already trust you.
      • Fewer downgrades. Catch customers who use less before they ask for a cheaper plan.
      • Fewer cancellations. Every unit of churned revenue has to be won back before you grow at all.

      Questions

      What is MRR?

      Monthly recurring revenue: the predictable revenue you expect every month from subscriptions, retainers or contracts. It leaves out one-off sales.

      What is net revenue retention?

      Revenue from last month’s customers this month, after upgrades, downgrades and cancellations, as a share of what they paid last month. Above 100% means you would grow even with no new customers.

      What is the quick ratio?

      New plus expansion revenue divided by contraction plus churned revenue. Above 1 you are growing; the higher it is, the less of your new revenue is spent replacing what leaked out.
