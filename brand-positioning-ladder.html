How the ladder works

Any brand can be positioned at different heights. At the bottom it competes on who makes it and what it is made of; higher up, on what it does for you, who it helps you be and what it stands for. Higher rungs are harder to copy and easier to love, but they only work when the rungs below are true.

  • Find the empty rung. If every competitor talks about features, a clear benefit or identity can stand out immediately.
  • Each rung proves the one above. The statement uses the rung below your claim as the reason to believe.

Questions

What is a brand positioning ladder?

A way to explore positioning options from the most concrete (company and product) to the most abstract (user identity and values), and to choose the rung that is both true and distinctive.

What makes a good positioning statement?

It names the audience, the category, the single benefit or idea you own, and a credible reason to believe. It should be internal and precise, not an ad slogan.

Which rung should I claim?

Usually the highest rung you can credibly own that competitors are not already occupying.
