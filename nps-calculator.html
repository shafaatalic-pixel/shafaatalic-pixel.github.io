      Reading NPS honestly

      NPS ranges from −100 to +100. Benchmarks vary widely by industry and country, so the most useful comparison is your own score over time, with its margin of error in view. A move of 5 points means little on a few hundred responses.

      • The number is a prompt, not the answer. The follow-up question, "what is the main reason for your score?", is where the insight lives.
      • Close the loop. Contacting detractors quickly often recovers the relationship, and tells you what to fix.

      Questions

      What is a good NPS?

      Anything above 0 means more promoters than detractors. Above 30 is generally strong and above 50 excellent, but benchmarks differ a lot by industry, so compare against your own trend.

      Why does NPS need a margin of error?

      Because it is estimated from a sample. With 200 responses a score of +30 might really be anywhere from about +20 to +40.

      Do passives count?

      They are not in the formula directly, but they count towards the total, so more passives pull both percentages down.
