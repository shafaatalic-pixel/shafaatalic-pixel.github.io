      Why ROAS alone misleads

      ROAS counts revenue, not profit. A 3x ROAS sounds strong, but at a 30% gross margin every unit of ad spend returns 0.9 units of profit, and the campaign loses money before you count the agency or the creative. The break-even ROAS is simply one divided by your margin, and it rises further once other campaign costs are included.

      • Know your break-even before launch. It is the floor every channel has to clear.
      • Platform-reported revenue flatters. Attribution windows and view-through conversions often count sales that would have happened anyway. Hold-out tests tell you the true lift.
      • First purchase is not lifetime. If customers come back, a campaign that loses money on day one can still be worth it. Pair this with the LTV to CAC calculator.

      Questions

      What is a good ROAS?

      There is no universal number. A good ROAS is one above your break-even ROAS, which is 1 divided by your gross margin. At 50% margin you need at least 2x; at 25% margin you need 4x.

      What is the difference between ROAS and ROI?

      ROAS divides revenue by ad spend. ROI divides profit by total investment. ROAS ignores product costs, so it always looks better than ROI.

      Should I include agency and creative costs?

      Yes, if you want the true break-even. This calculator adds them on top of ad spend when working out profit and the break-even ROAS.
