Every interaction is a trade

Whether it is a sign-up form, an app, a contest or a community, people weigh what it costs them against what they get. Brands tend to overestimate how interesting they are and underestimate the effort they ask for. The fix is usually on both sides: ask for less, and make one reward unmistakable.

  • Data is a cost. Every extra form field lowers completion; ask only for what you will use.
  • One strong reward beats many weak ones, which is why only the top three count here.

Questions

What is a value exchange in marketing?

The trade between what a brand asks of people (time, data, effort, money) and what they get in return (usefulness, fun, reward, status, belonging).

How is the score calculated?

Asks are added up out of 25. The three strongest rewards are added up out of 15. The ratio of the two, each scaled to 1, gives the exchange.

What score do I need?

Above about 1.3 the exchange is clearly positive. Between 0.9 and 1.3 it is borderline. Below that, expect low participation.
