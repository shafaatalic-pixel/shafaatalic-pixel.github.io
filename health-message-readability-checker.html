      Why plain language is a health intervention
      

      A message nobody can read changes nobody’s behavior. Low health literacy is linked to missed doses, skipped screenings and avoidable hospital visits, and it is far more common than most organizations assume. Writing at a 6th to 8th grade level is not dumbing down. It removes the friction between the advice and the action.
      

        
  
      • Short sentences. One idea per sentence. Aim for an average under 15 words.
        • 
  
      • Everyday words. “High blood pressure”, not “hypertension”. “Use”, not “utilize”.
        • 
  
      • Lead with the action. Tell people what to do first, then why.
        • 
  
      • Test with real readers. A formula is a check, not a substitute for five conversations with the people you are writing for.

      Questions
      

      What reading level should health information be written at?
      Patient and public health materials are commonly recommended to sit at a 6th to 8th grade reading level. Plain language helps everyone, including people with high literacy who are tired, worried or reading on a phone.
      

      How is the grade level calculated?
      It uses the Flesch-Kincaid grade formula, based on average sentence length and average syllables per word. It is a useful signal, not a verdict: short words can still be confusing, so read the jargon and long-sentence flags too.
      

      What is the plain-word draft?
      Your text with each flagged term swapped for an everyday word. It is a starting point, not a finished rewrite: read it aloud and fix any grammar the swaps create.
      

      Does it work for Bangla text?
      Partly. Grade-level formulas are built for English, so for Bangla the checker reports sentence length and flags long sentences, which is the biggest readability lever in any language.
      

      Is my text sent anywhere?
      No. The text is analyzed in your browser and never leaves your device. It is not saved in the page link either. The page only records that the checker was used, never what you pasted.
