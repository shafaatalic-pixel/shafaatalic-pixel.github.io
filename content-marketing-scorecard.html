        Questions

        How do I audit a content marketing program?

        Score it on a consistent set of essentials, such as audience clarity, goals, point of view, consistency, distribution, discoverability, conversion path and measurement, then fix the weakest areas first.

        What is the most common content marketing gap?

        Distribution. Many teams spend most of their effort creating content and very little getting it in front of the right people.

        How often should I re-score?

        Every quarter is a good rhythm: long enough to make changes, short enough to keep momentum.
